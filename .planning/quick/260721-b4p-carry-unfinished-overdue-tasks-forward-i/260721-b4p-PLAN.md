
Current filter in frontend/src/lib/agenda.ts (the bug):

```ts
const dayTasks = tasks.filter(
  (t) => t.due_date && t.due_date.slice(0, 10) === dateKey
);
```

`buildDayItems(tasks, events, dateKey, blocks)` is called by both `buildAgenda`
(single day = today) and `buildWeekAgenda` (7 iterations, offset 0..6).
`buildDayItems` has no notion of "is this today" — it only knows `dateKey`.
The carry-forward must therefore be gated by a new explicit parameter, NOT by
comparing `dateKey` to `new Date()` inside `buildDayItems` (that would make the
function non-deterministic and break the injectable-`now` test convention
established in 02-05).

Consumers of the returned items:
- `markTimeline(items, nowHHMM)` in nowView.ts assigns phase "upcoming" when
  `item.time === null`. Carried overdue items must therefore have `time: null`
  so they are not marked "past" by their stale original time.
- `groupAgendaItemsByParent` / `applyManualOrder` in TodayTimeline.tsx operate on
  the top-level list; carried items participate normally.
